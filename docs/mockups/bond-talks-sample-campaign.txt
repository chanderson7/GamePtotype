== talk 1  [hook: first_blood, about Wenna]
   Bread, ale and the fire. Wenna is drying a pair of boots on a stick. Garrick has said twice that they'll burn.
   It was Wenna's first battle with the company.
     WENNA: So that's a battle. I expected more of them.
   GARRICK: I was sick after my first. And my second. Nobody told me it stops.
     WENNA: I got 4. I counted.
   GARRICK: Nobody remembers their first. Only that they stood. Don't tell the men I said anything kind.
   GARRICK: Can we stand nearer tomorrow? Just a bit nearer. It helps.
     WENNA: Fine. But I'll not be slow about it.
   That's the end of it, for tonight. It's enough.

   At Highcliff nobody in the company cut down more than Mira: 6, by the men's count.
      MIRA: Wenna. 6. Tell the others. No. I'll tell them.
     WENNA: Then I'll put it about as 6 and let them argue.
      MIRA: There was one with a red shield. He was good. He isn't any more.
     WENNA: Then that's one you won't meet again. Eat something.
     WENNA: The men have started calling us by one name. I've stopped correcting them.
      MIRA: Let them. They've got it right for once.
   From Kitayama to here. The men have noticed, and have stopped remarking on it.

== talk 37  [hook: bandits, about Nasrin]
   Nasrin has given up explaining the tea. Mira drinks it anyway.
   The Red Waste reavers on the road by Highcliff. The company held the field.
    NASRIN: I don't like the hooded ones. You can't see what they're thinking.
      MIRA: Nasrin. Better than a quiet road. A quiet road pays nothing.
    NASRIN: One of them got close enough that I could smell him. Smoke and wet wool. I can still smell it.
      MIRA: Whatever you took off them, keep it. They'd have taken more off us.
      MIRA: I knew where you'd go today before you went. Annoying. Useful, but annoying.
    NASRIN: I saw. I'd have called for it. I think I would.
   They don't talk about Bramblemoor any more. They don't need to.

== talk 38  [hook: gutted, about Tamsin]
   Bread, ale and the fire. Tamsin is drying a pair of boots on a stick. Brann has said twice that they'll burn.
   Tamsin's men went into Highcliff 24 strong and came out 5.
    TAMSIN: I can't go back to my own fire. 19 empty places. I can't.
     BRANN: I don't know what to say. I'm sorry. I'm so sorry.
    TAMSIN: The ones left look at me like I did it.
     BRANN: Put them next to mine. Mine will look after them. And they'll look after me, I think.
     BRANN: My mother would have liked you. I've no idea why I said that. Forget it.
    TAMSIN: I'd noticed. I didn't want to say, in case it stopped.
   Whatever happens now, it happens to both of them. They've known that since Kitayama.

== talk 39  [hook: plain, about Nasrin]
   Brann has brought bread. Nasrin has brought dates. Neither has mentioned the other's.
   A fight by Highcliff, like a dozen others. The company held the field.
    NASRIN: I'm all right. I keep checking, and I'm all right.
     BRANN: Same. I keep counting my fingers like they might have gone.
    NASRIN: Highcliff. I've put it in my book. Everywhere we fight, I write down.
     BRANN: Write me in it. Beside you. Go to sleep.
     BRANN: I moved before you called today. I wasn't sure. I did it anyway.
    NASRIN: I noticed. It made the whole day easier.
   What began at Bramblemoor has become a habit. Neither would call it that.

== talk 40  [hook: hardest, about Nasrin]
   Nasrin has given up explaining the tea. Garrick drinks it anyway.
   At Marchgate nobody in the company cut down more than Nasrin: 6, by the men's count.
    NASRIN: I don't want them clapping me on the back. Can you tell them to stop?
   GARRICK: I'll tell them. Sit behind me. They won't come past me.
    NASRIN: I didn't decide any of it. My hands did. That's the part I can't sit with.
   GARRICK: Your hands did what they were drilled to. That's all. Here, take the bread.
   GARRICK: The young ones watch how we stand and copy it. I never thought anyone would copy me.
    NASRIN: I don't mind it. I thought I would.
