== Brann & Tariq, rank C, after the Raven ford  [hook: close_call, about Tariq]
   Tariq has given up explaining the tea. Brann drinks it anyway. Tariq has the bad leg out toward the fire.
   At Ravensford, Tariq went down with the Fenland cutthroats over them. Brann's Shields were close enough to drag Tariq clear.
     TARIQ: I keep thinking I'm still down. Stupid. I'm sitting right here.
     BRANN: You're out. That's the part I care about.
     TARIQ: I'd still be lying there if your lads hadn't come. I don't know what to do with that.
     BRANN: They know.
     BRANN: Same ground tomorrow. You on the left, me on the right.
     TARIQ: All right. I'd like that.
   Nothing is settled. But tomorrow each will know where the other is.

== Brann & Tariq, rank B, after storming Highcliff  [hook: walls, about Tariq]
   Tariq has given up explaining the tea. Brann drinks it anyway. Brann has the bad leg out toward the fire.
   They went over the wall at Highcliff on the same ladder, Tariq first.
     TARIQ: That ladder. I'll see it every time I close my eyes.
     BRANN: You went up it, though.
     TARIQ: I lost a left boot on the ladder. Can you believe that's what I keep thinking of?
     BRANN: Next wall we'll know what to do.
     BRANN: I moved my men before you called for it. I noticed you'd noticed.
     TARIQ: I saw. I'd have called for it. I think I would.
   They don't talk about the Raven ford any more. They don't need to.

== Mira & Hideo, rank C, after a plain fight at Thornwall  [hook: bandits, about Mira]
   Hideo is oiling a blade that doesn't need it. Mira has borrowed the oil for a shield rim and is making a mess of it.
   The Ash Hill brigands on the road by Thornwall. The company held the field.
      MIRA: The Ash Hill brigands. Not a proper fight. Barely worth the bandages.
     HIDEO: Mira. They always are. The roads don't clear themselves.
      MIRA: One of them had a good knife off a dead man of ours. I took it back.
     HIDEO: Quarter was right. We're not them. Pass the tea.
     HIDEO: Tomorrow, stay where I can reach you.
      MIRA: Fine. But I'll not be slow about it.
   That's the end of it, for tonight. It's enough.

== Layla & Kenji, rank A, after Layla's squad was gutted at Khalun  [hook: gutted, about Layla]
   Kenji has shown Layla how the sashimono is planted. Layla has shown Kenji how to get sand out of a boot. Both were needed.
   Layla's Spears went into Khalun 42 strong and came out 17.
     LAYLA: I can't go back to my own fire. 25 empty places. I can't.
     KENJI: Then we find who did it and we make the count even.
     LAYLA: The ones left look at me like I did it.
     KENJI: Then let them be angry beside mine. I'll put you on my right.
     KENJI: They put us in a song last night. Terrible song. Decent verse about you.
     LAYLA: I don't mind it. I thought I would.
   From Qasr Hamra to here. The men have noticed, and have stopped remarking on it.
