Bannerfall Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

Bannerfall collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What the game keeps, and where

Your campaign, your saves and your settings (music, text size and the like) are kept on your iPhone, in the game's own folder. They never leave the device by way of the game.

That folder appears in the Files app (On My iPhone › Bannerfall), so you can copy your saves yourself. If your iPhone backs itself up, to iCloud or to a computer, your saves are included in that backup like any other app's data. That is between you and Apple; the game sends nothing anywhere.

What the game doesn't do

  • It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.
  • It doesn't use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • It doesn't connect to the internet, and it has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version ever changed any of this, this page would say so, with a new date, before that version was released.
